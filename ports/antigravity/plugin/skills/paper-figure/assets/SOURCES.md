## Color books

`color-books.json`, `color-books.html`, `color-books.svg`, and the individual cards in `color-books/` are original authored resources. Paper figures are linked as design references in the JSON and [color guidance](../references/color-books.md); none are embedded or redistributed in the catalog. The HEX values are curated adaptations, not extracted author palettes. RLDX-1 Figures 2 and 3 were visually inspected on 2026-09-30.

The expanded collection has 16 books, a compact `color-books-index.svg`, and four sheets in `color-books/pages/`. The ten added studies were visually inspected on 2026-09-30: BLIP-2 Fig. 1, Flamingo Fig. 3, SAM 2 Fig. 3, Mask2Former Fig. 2, RT-2 Fig. 1, DINOv2 Fig. 3, Latent Diffusion Fig. 3, DiT Fig. 3, Mamba Fig. 1, and DINO Fig. 2. Each added JSON entry records the original URL and the observed color roles. DiT's main block and Mamba's selective-state diagram were read in their original SVG renderings; the catalog does not reproduce those images.
