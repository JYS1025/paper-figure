# Source attribution and comparison treatment

The published reference is Figure 1C (schematic portion only) of De Jonghe, J., Kaminski, T. S., Morse, D. B. et al., *spinDrop: a droplet microfluidic platform to maximise single-cell sequencing information content*, Nature Communications 14, 4788 (2023).

- [Original figure and caption](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-023-40322-w/figures/1)
- [Article and license statement](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-023-40322-w)
- [CC BY 4.0 license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)

For the comparison, the schematic part of panel C was cropped, internal text was covered by opaque neutral overlays, and the image was resized while preserving aspect ratio. The photographic panel was excluded. Graphical content was not generatively retouched, recolored, or redrawn. The generated figure received the same text-masking policy and a local detection/sorting crop. Both views were combined and flattened before model evaluation. Mask density and precise displayed object scale still differ.

The reference was chosen for the shared task of fluorescence-dependent droplet routing, before any evaluation verdict. It is a specific experimental device; the generated figure is a hypothetical methodology overview with unspecified channel dimensions, signal values, biology and electrode details. Their complexity and device-specific content are not matched.

In the delivered comparison, A is the generated figure and B is the published reference. This key and attribution were withheld from the evaluator until its initial response had been saved. The later labeled review is explicitly not blind.

The generated figure uses the skill's author-curated `olive-copper` color book. Its generator inspected [Mamba Figure 1](https://arxiv.org/html/2312.00752v2/selection.svg) for the use of colored routes on white and [MAE Figure 1](https://arxiv.org/html/2111.06377v3/arch.png) for concrete state representations. These design observations do not imply that either architecture or those authors' figures were copied into the new device schematic.
